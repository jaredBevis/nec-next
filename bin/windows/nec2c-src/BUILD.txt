nec2c 1.3.1 (https://github.com/KJ7LNW/nec2c, GPL-3.0), patched for Windows.
windows-port.patch shows the changes (sys/times.h, sigaction -> signal).

Cross-build from Linux:
  x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc -O2 -static -DPACKAGE_STRING='"nec2c 1.3.1 (win64)"' \
    -o nec2c.exe calculations.c geometry.c input.c matrix.c network.c shared.c \
    fields.c ground.c main.c misc.c radiation.c somnec.c -lm
